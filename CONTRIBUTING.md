# Contributing to Nexus AI

Thank you for your interest in Nexus AI! 🎉

Nexus AI is a free and open-source project, and everyone is welcome to contribute.

## Before contributing

- Please keep the project focused on Android + Kotlin + Jetpack Compose.
- Do not submit secrets, API keys, private credentials, or personal data.
- Do not copy proprietary code, assets, trademarks, or unpublished material from third parties.
- Clearly identify substantial changes that you authored.

## Contributions

By submitting a contribution, you agree that the contribution may be distributed as part of Nexus AI under the project's GNU General Public License v3.0, subject to the applicable contribution terms and copyright ownership.

Contributors retain the copyright in their original contributions unless they separately agree otherwise.

## Independent projects

Forks and applications based on Nexus AI may be independently operated. Their user data, content, business decisions, services, and activities do not automatically represent Nexus AI or its contributors.

Please preserve applicable copyright, license, attribution, and modification notices when redistributing GPLv3-covered code.

## Community standard

Be respectful, constructive, and honest about what code you wrote and what code originated from Nexus AI or other contributors.
