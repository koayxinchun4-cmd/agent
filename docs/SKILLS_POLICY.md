# Nexus AI — Skills Policy

## Our goal

Nexus AI is a free and open-source learning project. We want to learn from the open-source community, use Skills responsibly, and welcome developers who want to build with us.

## Third-party Skills

Nexus may use, reference, adapt, or learn from Skills published by other developers on GitHub when their license and repository terms permit that use.

A Skill being publicly visible on GitHub does **not** automatically mean that it may be copied, redistributed, or modified without checking its license.

For each third-party Skill that is incorporated into Nexus, we should record where it came from, its author, its applicable license, and the relevant repository/commit when practical.

## If an author asks us to remove a Skill

If the original author or rights holder believes that a Skill in Nexus should not be used, they are welcome to open a GitHub Issue and mention `@koayxinchun4-cmd` (or otherwise contact the project maintainers).

Please include:

- the Skill name or file path;
- the GitHub repository URL;
- the relevant copyright/licensing information;
- what material you believe belongs to you; and
- whether you are requesting removal, replacement, or clarification.

We will review the request in good faith. When a third-party Skill is not clearly licensed for the way Nexus is using it, or the rights holder requests removal and the request is reasonably supported, our default community approach is to remove or replace the material while we clarify the situation.

This is a community policy, not a statement that every removal request is legally valid. The applicable license and copyright law remain controlling.

## Attribution and provenance

Nexus should not present another developer's original Skill as if it were independently authored by Nexus. Applicable copyright, license, attribution, and modification notices must be preserved when required by the Skill's license.

When a Skill has been substantially modified, Nexus should distinguish the original source from Nexus-specific modifications.

## Independent responsibility

Using a third-party Skill does not make its original author responsible for Nexus, and Nexus does not claim that a third-party Skill author endorses Nexus unless they explicitly say so.

Likewise, a third-party fork or application using Nexus is independently operated. Its data, content, decisions, and behavior do not automatically represent Nexus or the original Skill authors.

## Learning together

The purpose of this project is to learn, build, share, and have fun with open-source software. If an author does not want their Skill included, we respect that preference and would rather remove it than create unnecessary conflict.
